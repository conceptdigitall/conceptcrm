## Creative laws

These apply to every brag video regardless of tone.

**Short.** 15–25 seconds. Not one second more without a reason.

**First-second movement.** Instant motion at 0.0s — punchy text slamming in, quick zoom on an error, an animated arrow pointing at the problem, or a moving cursor. Never start with a static logo, animated company intro, or "today I will show you".

**Kinetic typography as protagonist.** Large, animated words synced with the narrative pace. Words appear with weight, scale up smoothly, or highlight in accent colors ("LEAD PERDIDO", "12 SEGUNDOS", "SEM FUNCIONÁRIO"). Most viewers watch on mute; the typography must carry 100% of the narrative weight. Keep strictly to one big phrase at a time (3 to 6 words per screen).

**Visual change every 2–3 seconds.** Never hold a static frame. Cut, zoom, pop a new highlighted element, or introduce the next punchy phrase every 2–3s to sustain maximum retention and watch time.

**Show the real thing (Screen recording + motion graphics).** At least one scene must display actual product UI (WhatsApp conversation, CRM dashboard, n8n flow, landing page, spreadsheet). Pair it with motion overlays: animated cursor clicks, pulsing circles, directional arrows, or smooth focal zoom. Real software proof beats cartoon animation every time for B2B/SaaS conversion.

**Readable & accessible contrast.** Fast-in, then hold — never fast-in, then gone. Every sentence holds ~0.3s per word. All text must strictly maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background (e.g. #FFFFFF on #0624C7 / dark neutrals, #0A0A0A on #FCE026) so it passes `hyperframes check` accessibility verification.

**No generic SaaS language.** "Streamline your workflow" is banned. Use concrete, problem-solving phrases ("Perdeu leads às 23h", "Qualificou e agendou em 8s").

**Animated CTA.** End with an animated call-to-action button or simulated cursor click: "Comenta IA", "Salva isso", "Manda QUERO no direct".

**Pattern:**
```
Hook with kinetic movement (0–2s) → UI reveal / screen recording (2–5s) → Fast highlights / automation proof (5–15s) → Payoff & animated CTA (15–20s)
```
